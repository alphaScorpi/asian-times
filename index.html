Horological Discipline

Every Detail Matters.

Inside a watch movement, tolerances are measured in fractions of a millimetre. A single speck of dust or a slightly misplaced drop of oil can compromise amplitude and timekeeping.

At Asian Times, servicing begins long before a screwdriver touches a bridge screw. Each watch is examined closely to understand the condition of its dial, hands, case seals, and internal mechanism. We use properly dressed horological screwdrivers, non-magnetic tweezers, and protective movement holders to safeguard every surface.

I.

Thorough Inspection

Careful visual and acoustic evaluation of the movement, crown engagement, and gasket integrity prior to any intervention.

II.

Protected Handling

Dedicated trays, cushioned movement rings, and protective masking keep dials, hands, and polished lugs free from marks.
