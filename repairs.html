Precision Watch Repairs.

When a timepiece stops, loses accuracy, or suffers mechanical damage, careful diagnostics and component-level restoration bring it back to dependable operation.

Repair 01 • Manual-Wind Calibres

Mechanical Watch Repairs

From fatigued or broken mainsprings and worn barrel teeth to damaged balance staffs and slipped setting levers, we diagnose and rectify mechanical faults with component-level attention. Each repair is followed by thorough cleaning, fresh lubrication, and positional regulation.

  • Mainspring replacement and barrel arbor alignment
  • Gear train pivot and synthetic ruby jewel inspection
  • Escapement pallet fork and impulse adjustment
  • Hairspring centring and beat-error calibration

Repair 02 • Self-Winding Systems

Automatic Watch Repairs

Self-winding watches depend on a finely balanced oscillating weight and delicate reversing wheels to convert wrist motion into stored energy. When an automatic watch fails to hold its power reserve or produces rotor noise, we inspect the rotor bearing, axle, reduction gears, and slipping mainspring attachment.

  • Oscillating weight axle and ball-bearing restoration
  • Reversing wheel and automatic winding bridge care
  • Slipping-attachment mainspring and barrel wall inspection
  • Bidirectional and unidirectional winding verification

Repair 03 • Electronic & Hybrid Calibres

Quartz Watch Repairs

When a quartz watch fails to run even with a fresh battery, the cause often lies in oxidized battery terminals, a damaged copper coil, a stalled stepping motor rotor, or a fouled mechanical wheel train. We isolate electronic and mechanical faults systematically.

  • Electronic circuit board and coil resistance diagnostics
  • Battery leakage neutralization and terminal contact restoration
  • Stepping motor stator and magnetic rotor cleaning
  • Calendar mechanism and hand-setting train repair

Repair Methodology

A Careful Approach to Every Repair.

Rushing a repair risks secondary damage to delicate bridges, dials, and hands. We follow a disciplined six-stage bench protocol for every timepiece entrusted to us.

Stage 01

External & Functional Check

We document the condition of the case, crystal, dial, hands, crown threads, and bracelet before opening the caseback.

Stage 02

Safe Uncasing & Dial Protection

The movement is uncased using proper dies, and the hands and dial are lifted with protective shields and stored in dust-free trays.

Stage 03

Fault Isolation

Each sub-assembly—keyless works, gear train, escapement, balance, or electronic circuit—is inspected to isolate worn or damaged parts.

Stage 04

Component Restoration & Cleaning

Parts are repaired or replaced as agreed, followed by ultrasonic cleaning to remove aged oils and microscopic debris.

Stage 05

Reassembly & Micro-Oiling

Bridges and wheels are reassembled with calibrated screwdriver torque and lubricated at exact pivot points.

Stage 06

Timing & Final Observation

Once recased with dressed gaskets, the watch undergoes timing verification and power-reserve testing before handover.
