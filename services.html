Horological Services

Dedicated Care for Every Calibre.

From complete mechanical movement overhauls to routine battery changes, gasket checks, and sympathetic case detailing, every service at Asian Times is performed with methodical care in Lower Parel, Mumbai.

Service 01 • Full Overhaul

Complete Watch Servicing

Over time, fine lubricants within a watch movement age, thicken, or migrate, creating friction across pivots and gear teeth. Our complete watch service begins with the careful uncasing of the movement and separation of the dial and hands. Every component of the calibre is disassembled and cleaned in specialized ultrasonic solutions before being inspected under magnification.

During reassembly, worn components are addressed, and multiple grades of horological oils and greases are applied in exact micro-quantities to each friction point before multi-position timing calibration.

  • Complete movement disassembly and ultrasonic chemical bathing
  • Micro-lubrication of jewels, pivots, mainspring, and escapement
  • Multi-position timing regulation and amplitude assessment
  • Case cleaning, gasket inspection, and final quality verification

Service 02 • Traditional Horology

Mechanical Watch Servicing

Manual-wind and automatic mechanical watches rely on the delicate harmony of hundreds of interlocking parts—from the mainspring barrel and gear train to the escapement and oscillating balance wheel. Our mechanical servicing honours traditional watchmaking discipline, treating every bridge, screw, and jewel with unhurried precision.

For automatic movements, we pay particular attention to the reversing wheels, rotor axle, and winding efficiency so the timepiece maintains a dependable power reserve during daily wear.

  • Manual-wind and self-winding automatic calibre overhaul
  • Mainspring tension, barrel arbor, and winding mechanism care
  • Balance wheel poise, hairspring centring, and beat-error correction
  • Power reserve and winding efficiency verification

Service 03 • Electronic Precision

Quartz Watch Servicing

Modern and vintage quartz watches combine an electronic oscillator with a finely engineered mechanical gear train. Beyond simple battery changes, quartz calibres require periodic servicing to clean dried lubricants from the wheel train that can otherwise increase current draw and shorten cell life.

We test circuit board resistance, coil continuity, and stepping motor impulse strength while thoroughly servicing the mechanical wheels, setting lever, and keyless works.

  • Electronic module consumption and coil continuity diagnostics
  • Cleaning and lubrication of the mechanical reduction gear train
  • Battery contact remediation and corrosion removal
  • Quartz oscillator frequency and accuracy verification

Service 04 • Essential Care

Battery Replacement

An improperly changed battery can scratch a caseback, deform a gasket, or leave fingerprints on delicate movement plates. At Asian Times, every battery replacement is performed at the watchmaker's bench using proper case-opening dies, insulated tweezers, and high-grade cells matched to the movement specification.

Before closing the watch, we inspect the battery compartment for any signs of past leakage, clean the caseback seating channel, and dress the sealing gasket.

  • Scratch-free case opening using movement-appropriate dies
  • High-grade cell installation with non-magnetic insulated tools
  • Current consumption check and contact terminal cleaning
  • Caseback gasket dressing and secure resealing

Service 06 • Exterior Hygiene

Watch Cleaning

Mumbai's humidity, dust, and daily wear naturally accumulate between bracelet links, beneath bezels, and around the caseback. Left unchecked, trapped grit accelerates wear on metal pins and degrades rubber seals.

We separate the bracelet from the watch head—and, where needed, uncase the movement—so metal components can undergo deep ultrasonic cleaning, rinsing, and warm air drying before careful reassembly.

  • Ultrasonic cleaning of metal bracelets, clasps, and end-links
  • Gentle detailing of case recesses, bezel flutes, and crown guards
  • Removal of accumulated moisture, residue, and abrasive dust
  • Careful drying and inspection of all exterior fastening points

Service 07 • Protection & Seals

Water Resistance Checks

Water resistance is not a permanent property of a watch; rubber and synthetic gaskets dry out, flatten, and lose elasticity over time—particularly in warm, humid climates. Checking seals regularly helps protect the dial and movement from moisture intrusion.

We inspect the caseback gasket, crown seal, and crystal seating, replacing aged O-rings and applying silicone gasket treatment to help preserve your timepiece against everyday moisture exposure.

  • Inspection of caseback, crown, pusher, and crystal seals
  • Replacement of compressed or degraded rubber O-ring gaskets
  • Silicone lubrication of sealing surfaces
  • Pressure and condensation risk assessment

Service 08 • Case Refinishing

Watch Polishing

Refinishing a watch case requires restraint and respect for original geometry. Over-polishing softens crisp lug bevels and alters the character of a timepiece. Our approach focuses on sympathetic surface restoration that revives lustre while preserving case lines.

We mask distinct textures carefully to maintain the contrast between mirror-polished chamfers and fine satin-brushed surfaces across the case and bracelet.

  • Restrained surface refinishing that respects original lug geometry
  • Careful separation of mirror-polished and satin-brushed finishes
  • Removal of surface swirl marks and light everyday scuffs
  • Post-polish ultrasonic bath to remove all polishing compounds

Service 09 • Preventive Care

General Maintenance

Between full movement overhauls, periodic check-ups keep your timepiece performing reliably. If a watch begins running slightly fast or slow, feels stiff when winding, or has suffered an accidental knock, an early inspection prevents minor wear from becoming a major repair.

We perform demagnetization, timing checks, crown and stem inspections, dial and hand clearance verification, and general preventative adjustments.

  • Movement demagnetization and rate evaluation
  • Crown winding feel, hand-setting, and date-change inspection
  • Crystal, bezel, and caseback security check
  • Honest guidance on whether immediate servicing is needed
